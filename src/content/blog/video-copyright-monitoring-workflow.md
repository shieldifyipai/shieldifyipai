Video monitoring can produce exact copies, short clips, compilations, reactions, translated versions, altered audio, and excerpts embedded in commercial promotions. A match should enter a review workflow rather than move directly to removal.

## Register the reference work

Maintain the master file, title, duration, creation and publication dates, creator credits, ownership records, music and footage licenses, territory, and official publication URLs. If different parties control audio, visual, performance, or distribution rights, document those boundaries.

Create reference fingerprints only from material the organization is authorized to manage.

## Preserve the observed video

Record the platform, direct video URL, channel URL and identifier, title, description, upload date, duration, visible engagement, monetization indicators, and links. Capture the page and relevant playback segments with timestamps.

Do not download content by bypassing platform controls. Use approved evidence methods and preserve the source URL even when an authorized capture is made.

## Compare the audiovisual content

Document where the match begins and ends in both the reference and observed video. Note cropping, mirroring, speed changes, overlays, commentary, translated audio, or compilation structure.

An automated match percentage does not determine infringement. Review the amount, purpose, transformation, license status, applicable exceptions, and other relevant facts.

## Review channel and use context

Consider whether the upload is an official partner, licensed distributor, news report, classroom use, commentary, fan creation, commercial repost, or deceptive official-looking channel. Check internal rights and campaign records before escalating.

YouTube states that copyright owners or authorized representatives can submit removal requests and should consider copyright exceptions before doing so. It also offers different management tools depending on an owner's needs and operating capacity.

## Choose the appropriate action

Possible actions may include monitoring, contacting a license partner, using platform matching tools, requesting removal, monetization or tracking where available, or seeking legal review. The available choice depends on ownership, platform eligibility, territory, and organizational policy.

Keep the exact submission, declarations, affected URLs, reference work, representative authority, and platform response together.

## Handle counter-notices and disputes

Escalate counter-notices, ownership disputes, and claimed exceptions to qualified reviewers. Preserve deadlines and communications. Do not allow an automated workflow to resubmit a disputed claim without review.

## Measure program quality

Track matches reviewed, relevant match rate, time to review, evidence readiness, actions selected, counter-notices, retractions, outcomes, and repeated channels. Separate exact reuploads from contextual uses so leadership can understand the workload.
