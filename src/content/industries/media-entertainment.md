## Connect content monitoring with reliable rights information

Media and entertainment organizations manage a wide range of creative assets across creators, productions, campaigns, territories and distribution partners. Monitoring becomes more useful when each reference asset is connected to its owner, publication history and known permissions.

Shieldify IP helps organize public findings without assuming that every match is actionable. Reviewers can compare the observed use with the appropriate rights and licensing context before deciding how the case should proceed.

## Preserve the surrounding use

A screenshot of an isolated image or clip may omit information that matters. Evidence records can include the source page, account, URL, timestamp, visible attribution, surrounding text, duration, links and other contextual details available at the time of review.

## Route cases to the correct rights owner

Clear ownership and authority reduce delays in professional review and platform submissions. A structured workflow can assign each case to the appropriate internal owner, representative or counsel while keeping the action and response history connected.
