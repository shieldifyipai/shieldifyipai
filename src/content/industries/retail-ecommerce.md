## Protect product identity across a changing commerce environment

Retail and e-commerce teams operate across marketplaces, direct-to-consumer websites, social commerce, affiliates, search and advertising. That reach creates more places for customers to encounter confusing brand use, copied product content, suspicious listings or inaccurate seller claims.

A useful protection program starts with a reliable reference set: what the organization owns, what authentic products look like, who is authorized to sell them and which channels deserve the closest attention. Findings can then be reviewed consistently instead of being treated as isolated screenshots.

## Separate observable signals from conclusions

A low price, reused image or unfamiliar storefront can justify closer review, but it does not prove that a product is counterfeit or that a use is unlawful. Shieldify IP helps organize the visible facts, ownership information and review history so qualified teams can choose an appropriate response.

## Maintain a connected case history

Structured records keep listing URLs, seller details, screenshots, timestamps, product references, submitted actions and third-party responses connected. This makes recurring patterns easier to compare and gives stakeholders a clearer view of open, pending and resolved work.
