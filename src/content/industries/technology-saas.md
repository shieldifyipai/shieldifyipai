## Protect the identity around digital products

Technology companies depend on recognizable product names, trusted domains, official support channels, software interfaces and original content. Misleading profiles, copied product pages or confusing domains can create customer and brand risk even when the underlying scenarios require different legal or security responses.

Shieldify IP helps connect monitoring with a current inventory of official digital assets. Reviewers can compare a finding with the correct product, account, release or ownership record before it enters a case queue.

## Coordinate across specialist teams

Technology-related findings often touch legal, security, trust and safety, product, communications and customer-support teams. A shared evidence record reduces duplicated investigation and keeps observed facts separate from interpretation or response decisions.

## Preserve fast-changing digital evidence

Domains, profiles, advertisements, downloads and copied pages may change quickly. Capturing source information, screenshots, timestamps, visible claims and response history provides a stable basis for professional review and ongoing reporting.
