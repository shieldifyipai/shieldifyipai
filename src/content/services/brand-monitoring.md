## Build visibility across the places customers encounter a brand

Brand misuse does not stay within one platform. A lookalike social profile may link to a domain, advertise through a search or social campaign, and reuse content from an official website. Monitoring therefore needs a reference set and case model that connects findings across channels without treating every similarity as a confirmed relationship.

Shieldify IP helps teams define official brand signals, collect relevant public findings, preserve the user journey, and route higher-risk cases to the appropriate operational or professional review path.

## Prioritize customer-facing risk

Not every unofficial mention deserves the same response. Findings that appear to imitate customer support, request credentials or payment, use paid distribution, or repeatedly move between domains and accounts may deserve faster attention. The workflow records why a case received priority so decisions remain consistent and reviewable.

## Coordinate across brand, security, and legal teams

Depending on the facts, an appropriate response may involve a platform, registrar, host, advertising provider, internal security function, communications team, or qualified counsel. Centralized evidence and status history help these stakeholders work from the same factual record.
