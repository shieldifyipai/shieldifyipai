## Connect every finding to the original work

Copyright cases become difficult to manage when original assets, ownership records, licenses, external URLs, and platform responses live in separate systems. Shieldify IP structures these elements around a stable asset and case record so reviewers can understand what was created, where it appeared, and what authority exists to act.

The workflow can support single high-priority findings or recurring monitoring across websites, social channels, video platforms, and marketplaces. Coverage is defined by the works that matter most and the channels where unauthorized copying is most likely to create operational or commercial risk.

## Preserve context, not only the copied material

Evidence should show where the material appeared, how it was presented, who controlled the public source when visible, and when the use was captured. For edited images, cropped video, or combined content, factual comparison notes help professional reviewers assess the record without unsupported conclusions.

## Prepare for platform and professional review

Before any action, the workflow confirms ownership, authority, licenses, and possible issues that require legal assessment. Submission references, responses, counter-notices, appeals, and reappearances remain connected to the case so teams retain a complete operating history.
