## Turn marketplace volume into a prioritized review queue

Counterfeit monitoring can generate many suspicious listings, but a single signal rarely establishes authenticity. A mature workflow begins with current authentic-product and authorized-distribution references, then captures several independent observations before a finding is prioritized.

Shieldify IP supports the organization of public listing, seller, product, packaging, price, location, fulfillment, and recurrence information. The objective is to create a factual record for brand, investigative, platform, customs, or legal review—not to label a product counterfeit without sufficient evidence.

## Preserve seller and product context

Listings change quickly. Product titles, photos, inventory, storefront names, prices, and seller details may disappear or be edited. Consistent evidence capture helps teams understand what was visible at the time of review and compare repeat findings without losing the earlier context.

## Measure recurrence and case quality

Useful reporting goes beyond removal totals. Teams can monitor high-risk findings, review time, evidence completeness, recurring seller signals, platform responses, reappearance, and unresolved-case age. Final decisions remain with the relevant platforms and third parties.
