## Move from a documented finding to an accountable action workflow

Enforcement support begins after a finding has been reviewed and connected to the relevant rights and authority. The objective is to prepare an accurate case for the appropriate channel, retain exactly what was submitted, and track the response without guaranteeing a particular outcome.

Shieldify IP supports operational workflows involving platforms, marketplaces, hosts, registrars, advertising providers, and other third parties. Cases requiring legal strategy, jurisdiction-specific analysis, litigation, or regulatory engagement should be coordinated with qualified professionals.

## Match the action to the channel and evidence

Different providers require different forms, rights information, statements, supporting documents, and follow-up procedures. Case readiness review helps identify missing fields, duplicate submissions, unsupported claims, or ownership questions before action is taken.

## Report decisions transparently

Removal counts alone do not describe the quality of an enforcement program. Reporting should show which findings became cases, what actions were submitted, how third parties responded, how long cases remained open, why they closed, and whether related misuse reappeared.
