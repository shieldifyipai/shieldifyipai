## Evidence quality determines whether a finding can become a usable case

A URL in a spreadsheet or an isolated screenshot may be enough to flag an issue, but often not enough for consistent professional review. Evidence collection connects the public source, visible content, capture time, relevant rights record, factual comparison, reviewer notes, and later action history.

Shieldify IP helps organizations define repeatable evidence standards across marketplaces, websites, social platforms, search results, video, apps, and advertising. Required fields can vary by channel and case type while retaining a common operating structure.

## Separate observations from conclusions

Records should clearly distinguish what the source displayed from what a reviewer inferred. This makes the evidence easier to audit, reduces overstatement, and helps legal or platform reviewers understand how the case was prepared.

## Retain the complete case history

Evidence remains useful after submission. Platform references, responses, requests for more information, counter-notices, appeals, reappearance, and closure decisions belong in the same timeline so teams can report accurately and improve future case preparation.
