## Protect trademarks through a repeatable operating process

Trademark protection is most effective when rights information, monitoring coverage, evidence standards, professional review, and action history are connected. Searching for a brand name occasionally may identify obvious misuse, but it rarely provides the consistency required for a growing portfolio or a high-volume digital environment.

Shieldify IP helps organizations build a structured workflow around public online findings. The process begins with what the organization owns and where confusion or misuse is most likely to affect customers, reputation, distribution, or enforcement priorities.

## Review context before choosing an action

A matching word, logo, or product reference is a signal—not a legal conclusion. Reviewers consider the visible use, channel, seller or account context, product relationship, authorization records, territory, recurrence, and available evidence before a finding becomes a case.

This approach helps reduce duplicate work and avoids treating ordinary commentary, unrelated uses, or legitimate resale as automatically actionable. Where legal interpretation is required, the case can be routed to qualified counsel with a clearer factual record.

## Maintain evidence and response history

Each case can retain its source URL, screenshots, timestamps, ownership documents, reviewer notes, submitted action, third-party reference, response, follow-up, and closure reason. The resulting history supports consistent reporting and makes recurring accounts, sellers, or domains easier to compare without overstating what the evidence proves.
